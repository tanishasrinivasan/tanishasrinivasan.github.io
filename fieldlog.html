FieldLog

A voice recorder offshore technicians can use with gloves on, in the middle of the job.

Role
Product designer
Type
Personal project
Timeline
Sept 2026 – now
Tools
Zoo KCL (code-based CAD)

Where it came from

On a customer call with SubseaFPS, I heard that offshore technicians work 12-hour shifts and then write their reports from memory. FieldLog lets them record as they go: tap an NFC tag on a piece of equipment, press the big button, and leave a voice note tied to that asset.

Designing for gloves

  • A 38 mm concave record button you can find without looking
  • A separate flag button for anything that needs follow-up
  • A clip-on mount so it stays on the chest
  • A nonmetallic NFC window, kept clear of the clip
105 × 60 × 24mm enclosure
12separate parts
38 mmglove-friendly button

Catching a mistake

I used AI to help write the enclosure in Zoo's code-based CAD. The model looked clean, but it had placed the magnet mount and belt clip right over the NFC scan area, and metal near an NFC antenna can stop it from working. I caught it while checking the layout against how a technician would actually use the device, and moved them.
