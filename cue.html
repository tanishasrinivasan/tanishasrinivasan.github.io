What it does

Cue taps you when a speaking habit gets in your way, like filler words, rushing, or going too long without a pause. It learns how each person speaks across sessions and changes how it coaches them.

Why privacy mattered

In customer discovery, an EY partner who runs an executive coaching studio told me EY bans recording tools outright because client conversations are confidential. That stuck with me, and it's why Saylo processes everything on the device.

Where it went

Cue is where Saylo started. Professionals didn't want to wear it in high-stakes moments, so we pivoted to kids in speech therapy, keeping the same board inside.
