Tremor Wearable

A wrist wearable that picks up hand tremor and pushes back against it.

Role
Hardware lead
Team
2 people, Pie-Fi @ UCSC
Timeline
July – Dec 2025
Tools
Raspberry Pi Pico, C, SolidWorks

The idea

Hand tremor makes everyday things like eating or writing hard. We wanted a small device on the wrist that could sense tremor and work against it.

How it works

An MPU6050 IMU reads hand motion, and a Raspberry Pi Pico filters it for the 4–12 Hz band where tremor lives, so normal movement passes through. When it detects tremor, it drives a vibration motor with a spinning off-center weight to counter it. I wrote and debugged the C firmware.

Measuring it

We compared IMU readings with the device on and off, then confirmed it with on-person testing.

Four prototypes

Across four versions we changed the actuator, improved the fit, and moved from an Arduino to the Raspberry Pi Pico, with 3D-printed housings I designed in SolidWorks. We demoed the final version to more than 100 people at Pie-Fi Demo Day.
